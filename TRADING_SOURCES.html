13D/13G Filings — Activist Investors Taking Stakes
When a hedge fund acquires 5%+ of a company
FREE

When an activist hedge fund acquires more than 5% of a company, they file a 13D (activist, intends to influence management) or 13G (passive). Activist involvement often leads to buyouts, restructuring, spin-offs, or leadership changes — all bullish catalysts.

Best signal: 13D filing (activist intent) in a company you hold or are watching. Carl Icahn, Elliott Management, and ValueAct filings historically produce 15-40% gains.
Lag: 10 days
Frequency: As filed
Coverage: All public companies
